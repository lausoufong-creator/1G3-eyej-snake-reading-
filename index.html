📖 阅读文章

第一段

一天，有一位太太请客。大家围着桌子坐着， 一面吃喝，一面说笑。忽然女主人把女佣叫来， 低声吩咐了几句话。女佣听了脸色发白， 急忙跑了出去。

English: One day, a lady invited some guests to her home. Everyone sat around the table, eating, drinking and chatting. Suddenly, the hostess called her maid and whispered a few words to her. The maid turned pale and quickly ran out.
第二段

不一会儿，女佣端了一碗热牛奶， 匆匆穿过客厅，把牛奶放在了阳台上。 客人们都觉得奇怪，可是女主人仍然有说有笑。 又过了一会儿，女佣把阳台的门紧紧关住， 大声地吐了一口气。 女主人说：“好了，现在大家都安全了。”

English: After a short while, the maid carried a bowl of hot milk through the living room and placed it on the balcony. The guests thought it was strange, but the hostess continued chatting and smiling. A little later, the maid closed the balcony door tightly and let out a loud sigh of relief. The hostess said, “Good. Everyone is safe now.”
第三段

客人们问女主人到底是怎么回事。 她说：“刚才我们桌子底下有一条眼镜蛇， 不过，我现在已经把它关在门外了。”

English: The guests asked the hostess what had happened. She said, “There was a cobra under our table just now, but I have already shut it outside the door.”
第四段

客人们都吓了一跳。 女主人说：“眼镜蛇来的时候， 我不敢惊动它，也不敢告诉你们， 只好假装没有事。 因为眼镜蛇最喜欢喝牛奶， 所以我让女佣把一碗热牛奶放在阳台上。 它一闻到牛奶味，就会跟去。 女佣看见眼镜蛇到阳台上去喝牛奶了， 就马上把门关起来了。”

English: The guests were very frightened. The hostess said, “When the cobra came, I did not dare to disturb it or tell you about it, so I pretended that nothing was wrong. Because cobras like drinking milk, I asked the maid to put a bowl of hot milk on the balcony. When it smelled the milk, it followed the smell. When the maid saw the cobra go to the balcony to drink the milk, she immediately closed the door.”
第五段

一位客人说： “你怎么知道眼镜蛇就在我们桌子底下的？” 她说： “我能不知道吗？ 眼镜蛇就盘在我的脚上呀！”

English: One guest asked, “How did you know that the cobra was under our table?” She replied, “How could I not know? The cobra was coiled around my foot!”
第六段

另一位客人说： “你为什么不喊我们帮忙呢？” 她说： “我一喊，你们必定会慌乱起来。 大家一动，蛇受了惊， 只要咬一口，我的命就完了。”

English: Another guest asked, “Why didn't you call us for help?” She said, “If I had shouted, you would certainly have panicked. If everyone moved, the snake might have been startled. If it bit me even once, I could have died.”
